Our Story

Born from a deep love for Indian heritage, Aura Ethnicwear brings the finest handcrafted ethnic wear to modern women across India.

Our Mission

Weaving Dreams, One Saree at a Time

At Aura Ethnicwear, we believe every woman deserves to feel like a goddess. Founded in 2010 in the heart of Kolkata, we started with a small collection of handloom sarees sourced directly from weavers in Varanasi and Kanchipuram.

Today, we are proud to work with over 200 artisan families across India, bringing you authentic handcrafted ethnic wear that tells a story with every thread.

Our mission is simple — preserve India's rich textile heritage while making it accessible, contemporary, and affordable for every modern woman.

Our Values

🧵

Authentic Craftsmanship

Every piece is handcrafted by skilled artisans who have inherited their craft over generations.

🌿

Sustainable Fashion

We use eco-friendly dyes and sustainable fabrics, committed to reducing our environmental footprint.

💛

Artisan Welfare

Fair wages, safe working conditions, and community development for all our artisan partners.
